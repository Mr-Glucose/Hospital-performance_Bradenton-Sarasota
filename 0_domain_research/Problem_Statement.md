## Project: Hospital Performance & Readmission Insights — Bradenton / Sarasota

### Problem:
Readmission rates are a key indicator of hospital quality and care continuity. This project analyzes publicly available hospital-level data to compare readmission rates, patient experience, and safety metrics for local hospitals: Manatee Memorial, HCA Florida Blake Hospital, Lakewood Ranch Medical Center, and Sarasota Memorial.

### Research question:
How do readmission rates and patient satisfaction differ across these hospitals, and what factors might explain those differences?

### Motivation:
By analyzing this data, I hope to better understand local healthcare performance and identify opportunities for improved patient outcomes. This project will also help me apply data science techniques in a healthcare context and build a professional portfolio project relevant to hospital IT and data analytics roles.
