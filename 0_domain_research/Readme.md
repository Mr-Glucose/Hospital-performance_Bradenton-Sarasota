# Domain Research: Hospital Quality Metrics

## Background
Hospital readmission rates and patient satisfaction scores are critical indicators of healthcare quality used by patients, providers, and policymakers to evaluate hospital performance.

## Key Metrics Overview
- **Readmission Rates:** Measure the percentage of patients readmitted within 30 days of discharge, indicating care quality and discharge planning effectiveness.
- **HCAHPS Scores:** Hospital Consumer Assessment of Healthcare Providers and Systems - standardized patient satisfaction surveys measuring patient perspectives on hospital care.

## Local Context
This analysis focuses on four major healthcare providers in the Bradenton-Sarasota metropolitan area, serving a diverse patient population across Southwest Florida.
